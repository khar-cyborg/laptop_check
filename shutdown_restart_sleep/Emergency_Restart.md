# Emergency Restart

If your PC is frozen and you can't click anything, but you can still move the mouse, this is a secret restart method that bypasses the normal "saving work" screens and force-reboots immediately.

### How to do it:
1.  Press **Ctrl** + **Alt** + **Delete**.
2.  Look at the **Power button** in the bottom right corner.
3.  **Hold down the CTRL key** on your keyboard and click that **Power button**.
4.  A secret "Emergency Restart" screen will appear. Click **OK** to force a reboot instantly.
